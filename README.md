# The First Months of the War: A Calendar of Emotions

Daily emotional responses to the Russian full-scale invasion of Ukraine from February 24 to April 23, 2022, based on "The Diaries and Dreams of the War" archives at the Center for Urban History in Lviv. The project was supported by a grant from the Institute for Human Sciences (IWM) in Vienna as part of the Documenting Ukraine initiative.

## About This Project

This interactive calendar visualizes the emotional landscape of the first 59 days of the full-scale Russian invasion. Each day is coloured by its dominant emotion.
The data captures 10 emotions:

- Fear
- Anger
- Sadness
- Motivation
- Joy
- Admiration
- Shock
- Shame
- Love
- Envy

## How to Use

- **Hover over any day** – See the top three emotions for that day with their intensity scores.
- **Press Play** – Watch the calendar animate day by day. A metronome beat plays at a tempo that changes with each day's emotional intensity: faster for days with high emotional activity, slower for calmer days. The five most emotionally intense days also pulse visually during animation.
- **Click on any day** – Pause the animation and explore detailed emotion data.
- **Press Pause** – Stop the animation at any moment.
- **Press Reset** – Return to the beginning.
- **Spacebar** – Play or pause (keyboard shortcut).
- **M key** – Toggle sound on or off.

## Color Legend

- Fear – Dark navy
- Anger – Deep burgundy
- Sadness – Muted grey-green
- Motivation – Forest green
- Joy – Muted blue
- Admiration – Warm taupe
- Shock – Light grey
- Shame – Soft rose

Days with multiple dominant emotions are shown as patterns.
